High Court. May 05, 2026. (E063/064)

You cantnot auction without verification!.

The video focuses on a court hearing regarding a dispute over property verification and a potential auction involving homeowners, a bank (SBM), and an official receiver. Key topics discussed include:.

"Pay close attention to the judge's remarks on verification and data protection communication protocal.".

Verification Process Status:

  • Counsel expresses concern that no party has clearly stated if the verification process is complete.
  • While a report is expected, some parties indicate the verification is still ongoing.
  • The bank and official receiver clarify that they have concluded verification based on available information, though the process is challenging because parties are still submitting documents.

Concerns Over Auction and Redemption Notice:

  • Counsel argues that a redemption notice dated March 16, 2026, should be considered null and void because it includes properties that should be excluded from sale.
  • There is an allegation of "malice" against the bank, claiming they timed the end of verification to coincide with the expiry of the redemption notice to prevent homeowners from seeking legal recourse.
  • The court emphasizes that no auction can legally proceed until the bank knows exactly what it is and is not selling.

Communication of Verification:

  • The court recalls previous directions that each homeowner must receive individual communication from the official receiver and SBM regarding their status.
  • This individual communication was intended to address data protection concerns for both Creditors and homeowners.

Verification of Payments to Other Banks:

  • A specific issue is raised regarding payments made to banks other than SBM (e.g., Standard Chartered or DTB) before SBM became involved.
  • SBM notes it can only confirm information it has, while the court suggests counsel should engage directly with SBM to clarify these pre-2019 payments.

Court Directions and Future Rulings:

  • The court directs SBM to file its verification report by the close of business the following day.
  • The matter is scheduled for a "longer mention" on May 22nd.
  • On May 22nd, the court plans to deliver six composite rulings on applications related to contempt of court and the removal of the official receiver.
  • There will be no sale or auction of properties between the current hearing and the May 22nd date.
